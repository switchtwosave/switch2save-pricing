Supported files and reconciliation checks

This version reads the EPX statement layout with merchant cover, daily deposits, deposit summary, interchange detail and billing charges. Scanned, locked or other processor PDFs need separate review. The Valor export must have Status, Transaction Date and Time, TXN Type, Card Scheme, Base Amount($), Net Amount($), Store Name and Differential or Custom Fee columns.

Cash, declined and voided activity is excluded. Sales, fees, refunds and released funds must reconcile exactly. Daily settlement groups must match uniquely; the tool does not shift every date or discard the last day. Missing boundary transactions, ambiguous refunds, partial reversals and unfamiliar charges may require additional evidence. The report stays unavailable when a check fails, and the audit explains why.

The original statement supplies the business name, address, MID and month. Refunds are counted once. Surcharge recovery is separate from processing fees. The annual comparison includes the actual monthly fee; the effective-rate headline excludes it. No insurance/check-conversion savings are assumed.

Privacy and local processing

The reporting code and libraries are served by this STS site. Your files are read into browser memory and processed in a local WebAssembly worker. No statement or transaction data is uploaded, sent to AI services, saved in browser storage or retained by this page. Clear files to release the working session. PDF and audit downloads are saved only when you choose them.

All source matching is deterministic. Unusual fee arrangements, funding adjustments, ambiguous original-sale matches, more than four brands or more than nine service rows need a reviewed extension. A successful result confirms the implemented reconciliation checks, not an independent human audit.
