    Prices changed in your business?

    Record the dates and prices below only after you have applied them. Smart Track never updates your POS. Future steps remain a proposal.

    Cash, card, costs & rebates See how the result is built

    Receipts are sales money, not incremental benefit. Card proceeds subtract total processing fees. Earned rebates and paid rebates are separate; payment is never added to contribution again. No amount here is labeled available to withdraw.

    Daily, monthly & cumulative reports Recorded activity and price versions

    Effect by recorded price version

    Same-basket repricing holds item quantities constant. It does not prove pricing caused demand growth. Promotional discounts, refunds, missing price history and volume/mix changes can affect your result.

    Business & original pricing baseline Set up your starting point

    All dates are merchant-local calendar dates. Keep this baseline fixed once tracking begins. Changing it rewrites comparisons; a later price belongs in the change history.

    Applied price history Every recorded increase and reduction

    Use the proposal builder to record uniform or selective dated changes. Only changes you enter or import can be tracked. Old/new prices below are reconstructed in date order.

    Sales & refunds Manual entry or neutral CSV import

    Use item-level sales excluding tax and tips. One row per item, price, discount and tender. Split mixed tenders into correctly allocated rows. Refunds use a negative quantity and original sale date for price-version attribution; never enter the refund twice.

    Permanent lower prices go in price history, not again as a promotion. Discount dollars are positive for a sale and negative to reverse the discount on a refund. Leave original sale date blank for ordinary sales.

    Imports stay on this device. Existing line IDs are rejected, so re-importing the same rows cannot silently double sales. See the downloaded template for exact columns.

    Period fees, costs & reconciliation Enter known amounts; leave unknowns blank

    Enter one non-overlapping finance record per day or reporting period. Monthly totals are not guessed across days. Baseline amounts describe what the same period would have cost without the tracked changes.

    Operating costs in this period

    Compare matching baseline and actual cost categories. Smart Track subtracts each difference once for this period, not once for every price change. These inputs still do not provide full net profit or cash available to withdraw.

    Backup & local data Stored in this browser

    Download a backup regularly. Clearing browser data, closing all private/Guest windows, changing devices or using a different browser can lose access. This first version has no account, sync, scheduled report delivery or POS connection. Files are read locally and are not uploaded.

    Restore validates a complete backup before replacing local records. Download your current backup first. Keep files private: they include your business identity and financial inputs. Read the input and calculation guide.
